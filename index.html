About

I came to data work through physics. That means building the setup, writing the pipeline, and deciding whether the result is real. Before WashU I was a cadet at the U.S. Air Force Academy, where I coordinated mission planning for 100+ cadets. Before that, I founded a St. Louis nonprofit, Journey of Hope, and ran it for over ten years. I like work where careful analysis leads to a real decision.

  • Clean first. A data-derived threshold kept 85% of 10,000 instrument sweeps; the stock pipeline dropped 7 of 30 tickers and logged why.
  • Check against a baseline. 37 walk-forward test quarters, 83 features from a published study, and the coil's predicted field.
  • Report what failed. ML models that didn't beat a shuffled-label noise test, and dashboard bugs I found on a re-test.
