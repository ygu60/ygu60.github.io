Backtesting pipeline

Quant Alpha Research Pipeline

0.44OOS Sharpe
0.91Prob. Sharpe
−16.8%Max drawdown
10 yrsReal data

Problem

Can a simple statistical strategy for a small account show an edge that survives honest out-of-sample testing and transaction costs?

What I built

  • A data layer that pulls and caches price history from Yahoo Finance, then cleans up delistings, short histories, and reassigned tickers.
  • A vectorized backtest engine with a cost model and look-ahead protection, plus walk-forward validation that tunes parameters on training data only.
  • A composable risk overlay: volatility targeting, exposure caps, and a drawdown kill switch. I found and fixed two bugs in it along the way.
  • Unit tests for the engine, the risk overlay, and feature causality.

Results

  • Best strategy: short-term reversal with a regime gate. I cross-checked the gate across two lookback windows and against an independent Markov-switching model before trusting it.
  • Rejected a variant that looked great at one lookback and flipped negative at another, because it was overfit.
  • Tested ML signals (Ridge, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and a PyTorch MLP) with purged CV and a shuffled-label permutation test. None beat the noise floor, and I documented them as negative results.

Instrumentation · Data pipeline

ODMR Acquisition & Analysis Pipeline

~10kSweeps
10Field conditions
2→6Lines resolved

Problem

Measure how a magnetic field splits the resonance of NV centers in diamond. The signal is a fluorescence dip of just a few percent, buried in noise.

What I built

  • Acquisition code that controls a Keysight oscilloscope and function generator over PyVISA/SCPI. It captures about 1,000 triggered sweeps per condition.
  • A processing stage that filters, despikes, calibrates voltage to frequency, and averages the sweeps.
  • An adaptive N-Lorentzian fit that detects peaks automatically. It replaced two fixed-model fits that failed, and I documented why.
  • A read-only diagnostic tool that debugs trigger problems without risking an acquisition run.

Output

Reproducible figures (small multiples, ridgelines, 3D terrain), a full uncertainty analysis, and a LaTeX paper.

Undergraduate research · WashU

Predicting Aesthetic Ratings of Mosaic Art

982Images
60+Semantic features
2026Symposium abstract

Question

Which measurable features of a mosaic predict how beautiful people find it?

What I did

  • Built a dataset of 982 images with statistical and segmentation features at several levels.
  • Compared SAM2 and kernel graph cuts for extracting features at the tile level and at the semantic level.
  • Used LASSO regression, entropy measures, and spectral analysis to find which features predict ratings.

Output

An abstract presented at the Spring 2026 Undergraduate Research Symposium.
